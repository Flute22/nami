# Nami privacy policy

*Last updated: 20 November 2026*

Nami is a personal expense tracker. This page explains, in simple words, what happens to your information.

## The short version

- **Nami collects nothing.** There is no account, no sign-up and no login.
- **Nami shares nothing.** Your data is never sent to the developer or to anyone else.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking.**
- **Your data stays on your phone**, in an encrypted database.

## What Nami stores on your phone

Everything you enter: accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, bills and reminders, repeating entries, investments, and money you lend or borrow. Your profile details (name, username, profession and age) are asked once when you start. All of it is stored only on your phone, in a database encrypted with a random key kept in Android's secure storage. If you turn on App lock, your PIN is stored only as a salted hash.

Nami does not read your SMS, your contacts, your location or your notifications. It opens a photo only if you choose one yourself as your profile picture, and that picture stays inside Nami on your phone.

## What Nami sends over the internet

Only one thing: **downloads of public price files** for the mutual funds you choose to link (NAV data from AMFI, amfiindia.com). Each download is an ordinary request for a public file over HTTPS. It contains no information about you, your accounts or your amounts. Nami does not send your fund names, your holdings or any identifier.

*(Builds made by developers for personal use can also download public stock price files. The Google Play version of Nami does not.)*

## Files you import or export

- **Bank statements** you pick are read on your phone only. The copy Nami receives from Android's file picker is deleted as soon as it is read. Only the date, amount, a short name and the reference number of each entry you choose to add are kept, as normal entries.
- **Backups** (`.nami` files) are encrypted with a password you choose. Only you hold the password. If you forget it, nobody can open the backup. If you turn on Automatic copies, Nami keeps the last three encrypted backups in its own folder on your phone. They are removed if you uninstall Nami or delete all data.
- **CSV exports** are plain text so spreadsheets can open them. Nami warns you before saving one. Do not share them if they contain private details.

## Data safety on your phone

The app can hide its content in the recent-apps screen and block screenshots while App lock is on. Nami does not allow Android cloud backup of its data.

## Notifications

Reminders (bills, goals, budgets and others) are created on your phone. They need no internet. You can switch each kind off in Settings → Notifications.

## Deleting your data

You are in control. Delete one entry at a time, use **More → Backup & data → Delete all data**, or uninstall Nami. Uninstalling removes all of its data from your phone. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is nothing to delete on a server.

## Children

Nami is a general finance tool and is not directed at children under 13.

If the policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
